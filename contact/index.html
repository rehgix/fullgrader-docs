Contact

Is something not working as expected, is something missing, or do you have a question? Write to us and we’ll get back to you.

From the app

Go to Settings → Help and contact and tap Send an email. Your email app opens with the message started and your phone’s technical details, which help us find the problem. Nothing is sent until you tap send.

By email

Write to regixstudio.dev@gmail.com.

Send an email

To help you sooner, tell us

  • What you were doing and what happened.
  • Your phone model and Android version.
  • If it’s a scanning problem, a photo of the sheet. Cover your students’ names first.

Before writing, the answer may already be in the FAQ or the tutorial.
